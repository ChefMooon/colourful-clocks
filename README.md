Tell time in style with Pocket Watches and **five** clock designs! Mix and match Material Types, Glass Colours, Clock Hands, Pendulums, and Handbells to create the perfect decoration for your world!

## Clock Types

Each clock can be made from 41 different materials. With options to customize; Clock Hands, Pendulums, Handbells, and Glass if the clock can accept them. Clocks can also make a ticking sound.

### Bornholm Grandfather Clock
This clock comes in 3 parts: Base, Trunk, and Dial.
A clock will only function if all 3 pieces are placed together. The Hour hand will be at the top (12) at noon and midnight.
This version is styled after Danish Bornholm clocks.

**Base**  
A sturdy base. This can also be placed facing any direction for extra decorative uses.

**Trunk**  
This can be customized with Glass and a Pendulum. It can be crafted with a wood or glass door and can be changed once placed by right-clicking with the same clock material or glass type.

**Dial**  
This can be customized with Glass and a Pocket Watch. It can be crafted with any glass type and can be changed once placed by right-clicking with the glass type.

## Handbell
This item makes a chime sound when hitting entities and right-clicked. It can also be inserted into the Alarm Clock to allow it to chime at sunrise. The handle has ___ customization options and can be placed as a block similar to the vanilla bell. Chime pitch and swing speed are determined by bell material type 'weight', heavier metals will have a lower pitch.

![Handbell Showcase](img/showcase/handbell_showcase.png)

**Types:** Iron, Copper, Exposed Copper, Weathered Copper, Oxidized Copper, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, Quartz, Amethyst, Lapis Lazuli, Redstone, Emerald

## Pendulum
When placed inside a valid clock the clock chime at noon and midnight. Pendulum swing speeds and chime pitch are determined by metal 'weight', heavier metals will have a lower pitch.

![Pendulum Showcase](https://i.imgur.com/gUJwaqQ.png)

**Types:** Iron, Copper, Exposed Copper, Weathered Copper, Oxidized Copper, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, Quartz, Amethyst, Lapis Lazuli, Redstone, Emerald

**Note:** Exposed, Weathered, and Oxidized Copper Pendulums cannot be crafted. They oxidize when hanging in complete clocks and can be waxed to preserve the condition.
